Hey guys! I FINALLY finished my project for class. It is a ghost sweater. I was inspired to make this from previous CSCI assignments.

The light sensor will determine the time of day. The temperature sensor will determine whether the ghost is cold or hot. The RGBs will turn red if its too hot, and green if the ghost is in friendly mode. All four red LEDs will flash when it is cold at night or in a dark roomn to spook people. 

I used the following materials for this project:

2 RGB LEDS
2 red lilypad LEDS 
Conducive Thread
Light Sensor
Temperature Sensor
1 LilyPad LiliPo

1. Ask for more red LEDs because they can share the same thread and code. It will make the project look more aesthetically pleasing.
2. Find a sweater not made out of acrylic and wool because the electric current burns the sweater.
3. Devote more time to the project.
